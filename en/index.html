World

The world is a single painting

Painted in ink by Grandmother Ink and lifted aloft by Grandfather Wind—this is Palbang, the Eight Directions. Only things given a name remain in the painting.

Centuries ago, on the Night of Ink, the great map of the world and every record were drowned in ink. Lands that lost their names became ink mist, and from that mist mischievous yokai are born.

You are a Wanderer, a person named on no map. So you can walk anywhere—and wherever you walk is drawn again on the blank map in your hand.

“A map is not something you finish,
but something you redraw together.”

How you play

What you can do, not what number you are

You can't reach that place not because your level is low, but because you can't swim yet. See that glint on the cliff? Train your climbing and go.

遊

A blank map you draw on foot

Roads you walk, waymarks you light, fields you see from your kite—each fills in the map. Swim, climb and fly kites to open new paths.

品

Skills, not levels

Combat, life, exploration and arts: use a skill the way it's meant to be used and its rank rises. No need to pick a single path.

Novice9th…1stMastery

Read the stance

Combat about reading your foe's stance and answering it—not twitch reflexes. Built to stay readable on touch and gamepad alike.

生

A life lived together

Gather herbs, cook, play music and sit by the campfire. Better together, but never blocked alone.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of game is this?

A real-time online pixel RPG set in an East Asian fantasy world erased by ink mist, which you redraw on a blank map. Exploration comes first, with combat, life skills and arts alongside.

Are there really no levels?

Right. Instead of a character level, each skill has ranks (Novice → 9th … 1st → Mastery). Skills rise as you use them in their own way, and new paths open through what you can do—like swimming or climbing—not a level number.

Which platforms?

We're aiming for iOS, Android and PC (Steam), all playing in the same world (cross-play). The game is landscape, with touch and gamepad support planned.

When is release? Pre-registration?

Not decided yet. It will be announced in the news here first.

Can I play solo?

Yes. Some things are better together, but parties are never forced and solo play should never get stuck.

Can I buy power?

Our principle is that paid items never directly raise skill training or combat power. Details will be shared before release.

Which countries are these?

Fictional nations inspired by folklore from Korea, Japan, China and elsewhere in Asia. We don't use real countries, histories or place names, and no nation is portrayed as the villain.

The game itself is being made in Korean first; other languages are planned.
